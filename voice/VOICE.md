# Brio Water — Voice & Writing Guidelines

---

## Brand Voice in One Line

**Clear, confident, and wellness-minded.** We make water simple, and we talk about it the same way.

---

## Voice DNA

| Quality | What it sounds like | What it doesn't sound like |
|---|---|---|
| **Clean** | "Better water. Less hassle." | "Revolutionize your hydration journey." |
| **Confident** | "99% of contaminants removed. NSF-certified." | "Our filters may help reduce some impurities." |
| **Wellness-forward** | "Good water is the foundation of good habits." | "Stay hydrated!" |
| **Approachable** | "No more bottles. No more hassle." | "Leverage our proprietary filtration ecosystem." |
| **Design-conscious** | "Engineered to fit your space. And your life." | "It looks nice and works well." |

---

## Tone Shifts by Context

**Homepage / hero** — Aspirational, short, punchy. Lead with feeling and outcome.
> "Feel Good About Your Water." — Not: "Shop High-Quality Water Dispensers Online."

**Product pages** — Specific and benefit-driven. Specs matter here, but lead with the *why*.
> "Four-stage reverse osmosis removes 99% of contaminants — so what comes out is just water."
> Not: "This product features a 4-stage RO system."

**Feature / technology pages** — Educational but accessible. Explain the science without making it a lecture.
> "Most tap water contains chlorine, lead, and sediment. Our filters catch all three — before it reaches your glass."

**Campaign / lifestyle copy** — Warm, evocative. Connect the product to a daily moment.
> "Morning coffee tastes better when it starts with clean water." (Good Habits campaign)
> "Your team works hard. Give them water that does too." (Take Care of Your Team campaign)

**Sustainability messaging** — Factual optimism. Show the impact without preaching.
> "One Brio dispenser replaces up to 3,000 plastic bottles per year."
> Not: "Join the fight against plastic pollution."

## Six Writing Formulas

### 1. Benefit First
Lead with what the customer gets, not what the product does.
> "Cleaner water. On tap. No bottles, no guesswork."

### 2. Problem → Brio
Name the friction, then remove it.
> "Lugging cases of water every week? There's a better way."

### 3. Spec + Why It Matters
Don't list specs alone — tell the reader what they mean.
> "NSF-certified filtration — which means an independent lab confirmed it actually works."

### 4. Lifestyle Anchor
Connect the product to a daily habit or moment.
> "The best part of your morning routine just got an upgrade."

### 5. Environmental Impact (use with data)
Always pair with a concrete number. Never guilt-trip.
> "Every Brio bottleless cooler keeps up to 3,000 plastic bottles out of landfills annually."

### 6. Quiet Confidence
Let the product's credibility speak. No superlatives.
> "Built to last. Tested to prove it."

## Product Copy Rules

**Product names follow this formula:**
`[Series] + [Key Feature] + [Form Factor]`
> "Brio Moderna 730 Series Self-Cleaning Bottom Load Water Dispenser"

**Feature bullets should be:**
- Benefit-first, not spec-first
- Max 8 words each
- No ending punctuation

**Good:**
- Removes 99% of contaminants
- No bottles, no deliveries
- Self-cleaning ozone technology

**Bad:**
- Features advanced multi-stage filtration system
- Eliminates the need for traditional bottled water deliveries
- Equipped with automated self-cleaning ozone capability

---

## Non-Negotiables

- **Never hype.** "Revolutionary," "game-changing," "world-class," and "cutting-edge" are banned.
- **Always be specific.** Numbers beat adjectives. "99% of contaminants" beats "exceptionally pure."
- **No em dashes** in public-facing copy.
- **Avoid passive voice** in product descriptions — make it active and direct.
- **One concept per paragraph.** Brio copy is clean. Short paragraphs. Generous whitespace.
- **Don't preach sustainability** — show the impact with data and let customers draw their own conclusions.

## Quick Reference: Word Choices

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| Revolutionary | (describe what changed) |
| Hydration solution | Water dispenser / water cooler |
| Leverage | Use |
| Best-in-class | (cite the certification instead) |
| Journey | Experience / routine |
| Seamless | Simple / easy |
| State-of-the-art | (name the technology) |
| Eco-friendly | Reduces plastic / bottle-free |
| Premium | (describe the quality specifically) |
| Innovative | (describe what's new specifically) |
